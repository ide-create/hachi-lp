FAQ

Questions

Q. Which areas do you serve?

Nagoya city: Higashi, Chikusa, Showa, Meito and Mizuho wards, and nearby areas. Ask us if you live just outside.

Q. Do I need to speak Japanese?

No. Your requests go into your household's own handbook (the “Family Book”), and our app translates between Japanese and English for the housekeeper. Our team can also take your inquiry in English.

Q. What exactly is included?

Cleaning, tidying and folding laundry, and shopping errands. Ironing and cooking are not included. Childcare, babysitting, nursing care and medical acts are also outside the service.

Q. Is the same person always the one who comes?

Yes. One dedicated housekeeper visits on the same day and time each week, and learns how your home is run.

Q. How is the housekeeper chosen and trained?

Staff are Filipino housekeepers directly employed by our company, Sanesu Service Co., Ltd. They complete a two-day classroom course and accompanied visits with a senior staff member before they work on their own.

Q. What about my keys and my home when I am out?

Keys are held by our head office with a signed receipt and a log of every handover. After each visit you receive a report in the app, including a photo showing the door was locked.

Q. Are there any fees besides the monthly price?

No joining fee and no extra charges. Prices include tax. If you ask for shopping, only the actual cost of the goods is billed the following month.

Q. When does service start?

January 2027. We are taking early reservations now, and slots are limited by area and day of the week.
