  • Putting everything back in its place
02 ― KITCHEN & BATH

Kitchen and bath, properly, every visit

The same housekeeper, the same routine, every week. Nothing builds up.

  • Kitchen (sink, worktops, hob)
  • Bathroom and washbasin
  • Toilets
03 ― BEDROOM & LIVING

Bedrooms and living spaces, kept in order

Everything is done by the time you are home.

  • Bed making and changing sheets
  • Cleaning the living room and bedrooms
  • Four hours per visit, following your home handbook
04 ― HOME CARE

Restocking and waiting in, handled

No more "I need to buy that" or "I have to be home for that".

  • Checking and restocking household supplies
  • Shopping on your behalf
  • Waiting in for deliveries, repairs and inspections
05 ― REQUEST

One line in the app is enough

Your message goes straight to your housekeeper and our Japanese manager.

  • "We are low on detergent." "Please wait in next week."
  • A report after every visit, with a photo of the locked door
  • In English or Japanese
06 ― YOUR TIME

The time you got back, reported monthly

What you are paying for is time for yourself and your family.

  • Everything we handled, added up in hours

FAQ

Questions

Can you work while I am out?

Yes. We hold your key and finish while you are away. A photo of the locked door is sent to your app when we leave.

Which languages can I use?

English or Japanese. Your housekeeper speaks English, so you can write to her directly in the app. Requests written in Japanese are translated for her.

Are there costs beyond the monthly fee?

No. Only items bought on your behalf, such as restocked supplies, are billed at cost.
