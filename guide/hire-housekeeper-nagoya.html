How to Hire a Housekeeper in Nagoya
A Guide for Expat Families

November 5, 2026 | Operated by Sanesu Service Co., Ltd. (30 years employing foreign workers in Japan)

Hiring help at home in Japan works a little differently from many other countries. Here is what to know before you start, and what to ask any company you are considering.

Two kinds of housekeeping service

Most services in Japan fall into one of two styles.

Hourly or one-off visitsRegular, dedicated housekeeper
How it worksYou book a time slot. Whoever is available comes.The same person visits on the same day and time each week.
Good forOccasional deep cleaning, moving, trying a serviceKeeping a home running week after week
Trade-offYou may need to explain your home each timeUsually a monthly fee and a longer commitment

If you want your home kept up regularly, a dedicated housekeeper saves you the repeated explaining. Hachi Life is built around this model.

Questions to ask before you sign

  • What is included, and what is not? Ironing, cooking, childcare and care for family members are handled differently by each company. Ask for the list in writing.
  • How is the price built? Check for joining fees, transport fees, cancellation fees and the cost of shopping. Confirm whether prices include tax.
  • Who is the housekeeper? Ask whether staff are employed by the company or are independent, and what training they receive.
  • How are keys handled? You want a signed receipt, a log, and a way to confirm the door was locked.
  • Is there insurance? Ask what happens if something is damaged.
  • Can you communicate in your language? This matters more than most people expect. See below.

Getting started well

  1. Start with the tasks that bother you most, such as the kitchen, bathroom and laundry.
  2. Put away valuables and tell your housekeeper which rooms or items to leave alone.
  3. Be at home for the first visit if you can. At Hachi Life, a senior staff member joins the first two visits.
  4. Give feedback early. Small adjustments in the first weeks make the biggest difference.
